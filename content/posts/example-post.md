# Welcome to My Blog

This is an example blog post demonstrating the POSSE (Publish on Own Site, Syndicate Elsewhere) system.

## What is POSSE?

POSSE is a publishing strategy where you:

1. **Publish first** on your own website
2. **Syndicate** to other platforms (LinkedIn, Twitter, Dev.to, Medium)
3. **Link back** to your original post

## Benefits

- **Ownership**: Your content lives on your domain
- **Control**: You decide how and where to share
- **Discovery**: Links back to your site help with SEO
- **Independence**: Not locked into any platform

## Code Example

Here's a simple example:

\`\`\`typescript
function publishPost(post: Post) {
  // 1. Publish on own site
  publishOnSite(post);
  
  // 2. Syndicate to platforms
  syndicateToPlatforms(post, ['linkedin', 'twitter', 'devto']);
}
\`\`\`

## Conclusion

POSSE gives you the best of both worlds: ownership and reach.
